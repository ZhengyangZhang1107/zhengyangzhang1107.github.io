![FAST-GS overview](/images/publications/fast-gs-overview.png)

FAST-GS addresses trajectory drift and high-frequency motion degradation in dynamic 4D Gaussian Splatting. It decomposes motion into frequency-based sinusoidal components to jointly capture low-frequency global trajectories and high-frequency local details, while preserving real-time rendering capability.

The paper is available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01958) and has been accepted by **ICASSP 2026**.
