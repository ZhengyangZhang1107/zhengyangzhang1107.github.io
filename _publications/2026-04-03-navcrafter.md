![NavCrafter overview](/images/publications/navcrafter-overview.png)

NavCrafter creates flexible 3D scenes from a single image by combining camera-controllable novel-view video synthesis with a geometry-aware expansion strategy and an enhanced 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction pipeline.

The paper is available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02828) and has been accepted by **ICRA 2026**.
