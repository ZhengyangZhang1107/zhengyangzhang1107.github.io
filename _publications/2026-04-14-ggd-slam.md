![GGD-SLAM overview](/images/publications/ggd-slam-overview.png)

GGD-SLAM addresses dynamic-scene degradation in monocular 3DGS SLAM by separating static and dynamic components with a generalizable motion model, reducing the impact of dynamic distractors, and improving dense reconstruction quality in real-world dynamic scenes.

The paper is available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12837) and has been accepted by **ICRA 2026**.
